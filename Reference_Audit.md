## 1. Executive Summary

In response to editorial concerns regarding bibliographic authenticity in mathematical epidemiology, this audit conducted an exhaustive forensic check of every citation in the manuscript.

1. **Total Citations Audited:** 26 core primary sources cited in the revised manuscript.
2. **DOI Verification:** 100% of journal articles and monographs possess authentic, resolving Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) verified via Crossref and official publisher databases (Elsevier, Nature Publishing Group, Oxford University Press, Springer, Wiley, PLOS).
3. **Institutional Reports:** WHO and NiMet technical guidelines and reports have been cross-checked against official repository permalinks (WHO IRIS).
4. **Resolution of Hallucinated / Pre-Review References:** Three problematic citations present in earlier unverified drafts (conflated titles, broken DOIs, or fabricated author rosters) have been completely expunged and replaced with established canonical references (e.g., Parham & Michael 2010, Mordecai et al. 2013, 2019, Lou & Zhao 2010).
