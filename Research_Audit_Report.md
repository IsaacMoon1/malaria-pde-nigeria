## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

This forensic audit rigorously evaluates the mathematical formulations, computational implementation, statistical procedures, and manuscript claims in the research project titled **"Statistical Validation and Uncertainty Quantification of a Reaction-Diffusion PDE Model for Malaria Intervention Timing: A Multi-Zone Synthetic Benchmark"**.

Prior versions suffered from discrepancies between written claims and code implementation (notably in data provenance, numerical stability of aquatic stages, acquired immunity erosion during prophylaxis, uncalibrated synthetic data generation, and unpropagated decision uncertainty). Under the Master Scientific Reconstruction protocol, every equation, parameter, algorithm, table, figure, and citation was inspected, stress-tested, and reconstructed.
